# GHA++ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

GHA++ improves GitHub Actions job-log viewing. It is independent of GitHub.

## Information the extension handles

GHA++ uses GitHub page URLs, repository and Actions job information, log contents,
account identifiers, and authentication information to display logs and live
updates using your existing GitHub access. Logs may contain personal or
confidential information. The extension also handles viewer interactions and
your enable/disable preference to operate the interface.

## Where information goes

Logs are processed in your browser. To retrieve logs and updates, GHA++ connects
securely to GitHub and its log-storage provider, Microsoft Azure. These services
receive the resource and authentication information needed for the requests and
ordinary connection information, such as your IP address. Their privacy policies
apply to their handling of requests.

Chrome may sync your enable/disable preference through your Google account,
according to your browser settings. Logs and GitHub credentials are not synced.
GHA++ does not automatically send user data to the developer and includes no
advertising or analytics service.

## Retention and controls

Viewer data is retained for the page session; browser caching is controlled by
your browser. Your preference remains until changed or cleared. You can turn
GHA++ off from its popup, or disable or remove it in Chrome.

## Use and sharing restrictions

GHA++'s use of user data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including
the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide and troubleshoot the
viewer, not for advertising, profiling, sale, or lending decisions. Data is not
sold or transferred to data brokers.

If you send a bug report, its recipients receive the information you include.
GitHub issues are public; do not include secrets or private logs.

## Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the
[GHA++ project](https://github.com/mikekap/ghaplusplus). This policy will be updated
when the extension's data practices change, with the update date shown above.
